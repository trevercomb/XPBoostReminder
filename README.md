# Sleeping Bag Reminder

A tiny WoW Forever addon: alerts you (sound, raid-warning text, chat line) when your
Cozy Sleeping Bag comes off cooldown, at login/reload if it's ready, and every 5 minutes
until you use it. Alerts are held during combat (and while dead) and fire when it ends.

- `/sbr` — turn it on or off
- `/sbr test` — play the alert
- `/sbr status` — on/off, and whether the bag was found and is ready
