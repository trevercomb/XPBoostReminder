**Sleeping Bag Reminder** reminds you to use your **Cozy Sleeping Bag** whenever it's off cooldown.

The sleeping bag's rested experience bonus lasts 2 hours, but its cooldown is only 1 hour, so it's worth unfurling again as soon as it's ready. This addon makes sure you don't forget.

## What it does

- When the bag comes off cooldown: a sound, an on-screen warning and a chat line.
- At login or `/reload`, if the bag is already ready.
- A repeat reminder every 5 minutes until you use it.
- Never interrupts a fight: reminders wait until combat ends, or until you're alive again.

It finds the bag in your bags by name, so there's nothing to set up.

## Commands

- `/sbr`: turn reminders on or off
- `/sbr test`: play the alert
- `/sbr status`: whether it's on, and how long the bag's cooldown has left

`/sleepingbag` works too.
