**XP Boost Reminder** keeps your experience buffs up while you level in WoW Forever: the Cozy Sleeping Bag's Well-Rested, and XP food like Well Fed.

When a boost needs attention you get a sound, an on-screen warning, a chat line and a button to fix it in one click. It never interrupts a fight: reminders wait until combat ends, or until you're alive again.

## Cozy Sleeping Bag

Sleeping in the bag gives Well-Rested, up to 3 stacks for 2 hours. The bag's cooldown is 1 hour.

- **Below 3 stacks, or no buff:** you're reminded as soon as the bag is off cooldown, so you can build the stacks up.
- **At 3 stacks:** you're reminded when the buff has 10 minutes left, so you sleep again and keep the stacks before they run out.

## XP food and other XP buffs

When Well Fed, or any other buff whose tooltip mentions experience, wears off (or you lose it by dying), you're reminded to put it back up. Up to 3 reminders, 5 minutes apart, so it stops if you're out of food.

## Click to use

A button appears for the reminder that's due: **click to eat** or **click to sleep**.

- For food, it remembers what you last ate before the buff appeared, and otherwise finds XP food in your bags.
- **Right-click** means "not now": it hides the button and silences that reminder, for food until the buff is back and wears off again, for the sleeping bag until you next use it.
- Buttons hide during combat, since the game doesn't allow changing them then.

## Commands

- `/xpb`: turn reminders on or off
- `/xpb test`: play the alerts
- `/xpb status`: the bag's cooldown, your Well-Rested stacks and time left, your other XP buffs, and which food the eat button would use

`/xpboost` works too. No setup needed.
